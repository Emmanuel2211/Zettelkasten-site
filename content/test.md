# Prueba de pseudocódigo: Quicksort

Mismo algoritmo (corregido, versión CLRS) en tres formatos, sin plugin.

## 1. LaTeX puro (MathJax)

> [!example]+ Algoritmo. (Quicksort)
> $$
> \begin{array}{rl}
> \scriptstyle 1: & \textbf{procedure } \textsf{Quicksort}(A, p, r) \\
> \scriptstyle 2: & \quad \textbf{if } p < r \textbf{ then} \\
> \scriptstyle 3: & \quad\quad q \gets \textsf{Partition}(A, p, r) \\
> \scriptstyle 4: & \quad\quad \textsf{Quicksort}(A, p, q-1) \\
> \scriptstyle 5: & \quad\quad \textsf{Quicksort}(A, q+1, r) \\
> \scriptstyle 6: & \quad \textbf{end if} \\
> \scriptstyle 7: & \textbf{end procedure} \\[0.8em]
> \scriptstyle 8: & \textbf{procedure } \textsf{Partition}(A, p, r) \\
> \scriptstyle 9: & \quad x \gets A[r] \\
> \scriptstyle 10: & \quad i \gets p - 1 \\
> \scriptstyle 11: & \quad \textbf{for } j \gets p \textbf{ to } r-1 \textbf{ do} \\
> \scriptstyle 12: & \quad\quad \textbf{if } A[j] \leq x \textbf{ then} \\
> \scriptstyle 13: & \quad\quad\quad i \gets i + 1 \\
> \scriptstyle 14: & \quad\quad\quad \text{intercambiar } A[i] \text{ con } A[j] \\
> \scriptstyle 15: & \quad\quad \textbf{end if} \\
> \scriptstyle 16: & \quad \textbf{end for} \\
> \scriptstyle 17: & \quad \text{intercambiar } A[i+1] \text{ con } A[r] \\
> \scriptstyle 18: & \quad \textbf{return } i + 1 \\
> \scriptstyle 19: & \textbf{end procedure}
> \end{array}
> $$

\caption{Quicksort}
\SetKwFunction{QuickSort}{Quicksort}
\SetKwFunction{Partition}{Partition}
\Input{An array $A[0..n-1]$ and indices $0 \leq p, r \leq n-1$}
\Output{$A[p..r]$ sorted in place}
\BlankLine
\tcp{initial call: \QuickSort{$A, 0, n-1$}}
\Fn{\QuickSort{$A, p, r$}}{
  \If{$p < r$}{
    $q \gets$ \Partition{$A, p, r$}\;
    \QuickSort{$A, p, q-1$}\;
    \QuickSort{$A, q+1, r$}\;
  }
}
\BlankLine
\Fn{\Partition{$A, p, r$}}{
  $x \gets A[r]$ \tcp*{pivot}
  $i \gets p - 1$\;
  \For{$j \gets p$ \KwTo $r - 1$}{
    \If{$A[j] \leq x$}{
      $i \gets i + 1$\;
      exchange $A[i]$ with $A[j]$\;
    }
  }
  exchange $A[i+1]$ with $A[r]$ \tcp*{pivot to its final place}
  \Return{$i + 1$}\;
}

## 3. Código real (Python)

```python title=QuickSort
def quicksort(A, p, r):
    if p < r:
        q = partition(A, p, r)
        quicksort(A, p, q - 1)
        quicksort(A, q + 1, r)

def partition(A, p, r):
    x = A[r]
    i = p - 1
    for j in range(p, r):
        if A[j] <= x:
            i += 1
            A[i], A[j] = A[j], A[i]
    A[i + 1], A[r] = A[r], A[i + 1]
    return i + 1

# uso: quicksort(A, 0, len(A) - 1)
```
