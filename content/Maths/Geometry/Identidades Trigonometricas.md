#### Fundamentales
$$
\begin{align}
\sin \theta = \frac{1}{\csc \theta} \\[1em]
\cos \theta = \frac{1}{\sec \theta} \\[1em]
\tan \theta = \frac{1}{\cot \theta} \\[1em]
\tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \\[1em]
\cot \theta = \frac{\cos \theta}{\sin \theta}
\end{align}
$$
#### Identidades Pitagóricas
Derivan del [[Teorema de Pitagoras]] unitario:
#### Paridad (simetría)
Pares:
$$
\begin{align}
\cos (-\theta) = \cos \theta \\
\sec(-\theta) = \sec \theta
\end{align}
$$
Impares:
$$
\begin{align}
\sin(-\theta) &= -\sin \theta \\
\tan (-\theta) &= -\tan \theta \\
\csc(-\theta)&=-\csc \theta \\
\cot(-\theta) &= - \cot \theta
\end{align}
$$

#### Fórmulas de Cofunción (Desplazamiento de Fase)
Desplazar el ángulo en $\frac{\pi}{2}$ radianes.
$$
\begin{array}{c}{{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cos(\theta)}}\\ {{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\sin(\theta)}}\\ {{\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\csc(\theta)}}\\ {{\sec\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\sec(\theta)}}\\ {{\cot\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\tan(\theta)}}\end{array}
$$
#### Suma y Diferencia de ángulos
$$\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin(\alpha)\cos(\beta)\pm\cos(\alpha)\sin(\beta)$$
$$\cos(\alpha\pm\beta)=\cos(\alpha)\cos(\beta)\mp\sin(\alpha)\sin(\beta)$$
$$\tan(\alpha\pm\beta)={\frac{\tan(\alpha)\pm\tan(\beta)}{1\mp\tan(\alpha)\tan(\beta)}}$$
#### Ángulo doble
$$\sin(2\theta)=2\sin(\theta)\cos(\theta)$$
$$
\begin{align}
\cos(2\theta) &= \cos^{2}(\theta)-\sin^{2}(\theta) \\ 
\cos(2\theta) &= 2\cos^{2}(\theta)-1 \\ 
\cos(2\theta) &= 1-2\sin^{2}(\theta)
\end{align}
$$
$$
\tan(2\theta)=\frac{2\tan(\theta)}{1-\tan^{2}(\theta)}
$$


#### Reducción de potencias
$$\sin^{2}(\theta)={\frac{1-\cos(2\theta)}{2}}$$
$$\cos^{2}(\theta)={\frac{1+\cos(2\theta)}{2}}$$
$$\tan^{2}(\theta)={\frac{1-\cos(2\theta)}{1+\cos(2\theta)}}$$

#### Producto a Suma
$$\sin(\alpha)\sin(\beta)=\frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha-\beta)-\cos(\alpha+\beta)]$$
$$\cos(\alpha)\cos(\beta)=\frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha-\beta)+\cos(\alpha+\beta)]$$
$$\sin(\alpha)\cos(\beta)=\frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)]$$
$$\cos(\alpha)\sin(\beta)=\frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha+\beta)-\sin(\alpha-\beta)]$$



#### Suma a Producto
$$\sin(\alpha)+\sin(\beta)=2\sin\left({\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}}\right)\cos\left({\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}}\right)$$
$$\sin(\alpha)-\sin(\beta)=2\cos\left({\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}}\right)\sin\left({\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}}\right)$$$$\cos(\alpha)+\cos(\beta)=2\cos\left({\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}}\right)\cos\left({\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}}\right)$$
$$\cos(\alpha)-\cos(\beta)=-2\sin\left({\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}}\right)\sin\left({\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}}\right)$$


#### Geometría Plana (Leyes de triángulos generales)
##### Ley de los senos
$$
\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}
$$
##### Ley de los cosenos (Generalización del teorema de pitágoras)
$$
c^{2}= a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos C
$$
##### Ley de las tangentes
$$
\frac{a-b}{a+b}=\frac{\tan\left( \frac{A-B}{2} \right)}{\tan \left(\frac{A+B}{2} \right)}
$$
