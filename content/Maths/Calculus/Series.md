Una lista de series y sumas finitas 

> [!theorem] **Teorema.** (Series Finitas)
> - Suma de Gauss:
> $$
> \sum_{x=1}^{n} x = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
> $$
> - Teorema de Nicomaco:
> $$
> \sum_{x=1}^{n} x^{3} = \left(  \sum_{x=1}^{n} x \right)^{2}
> $$


> [!theorem] **Teorema.** (Series Numerables)
> - Serie Armónica:
> $$
> \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{x} \to \infty
> $$
> - Serie Geométrica:
> $$
> \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} a r ^{x} = a + ar + ar^{2} + ar^{3} + \dots
> $$
> Si $\left| r \right| < 1$, ent.
> $$
> \sum_{x=1}^{\infty} ar^{x} = \frac{r}{1-r}
> $$
> $$
> \sum_{x=0}^{\infty} ar^{x} = \frac{a}{1-r}
> $$
