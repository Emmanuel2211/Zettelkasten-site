> [!theorem] **Definición.** (chida)
>
> Dados $a>0$ y $x \in \mathbb{R}$ definimos
> $$
> a^{x} := e^{x \log (a)}
> $$

> [!observation]+ **Observación**
>
> 1. 
> $$
> \begin{align}
> \log(a^{x}) = \log(e^{x\log(a)}) = x \log (a) \\
> \implies x = \frac{\log(a^{x})}{\log(a)}
> \end{align}
> $$
> 2. Escribiendo $y:= a^{x}$ se define
> $$
> \log_{a}^{y} := \frac{\log(y)}{\log(a)}
> $$
> 3.
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}a^{x} = a^{x} \log(a)
> $$
